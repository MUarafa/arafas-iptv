Arafa's IPTV — Privacy policy

The app does not collect, sell or share personal data, and has no analytics or ads.

The server address, username and password you enter are stored only on your TV and sent only to that server, to sign in and load your channels. Viewing progress, favourites and settings are stored only on your TV.

The Free TV section loads a public channel list from iptv-org.github.io.

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.
